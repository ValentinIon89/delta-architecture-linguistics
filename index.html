Abstract

This research proposes the Δ operator as a unified formal framework for describing grammatical transformations. It distinguishes macrostructural correspondences from microstructural organization and classifies transformation patterns into eight operatorial regimes.

The approach aims to support comparative linguistic description and further investigation into formal representation and computational applications.

About the Research

The Δ architecture explores whether a unified operatorial notation can describe different kinds of grammatical transformation while preserving the distinctions between them. Its macrostructural level represents relations between configurations; its microstructural level describes the internal changes involved.

These formal proposals are part of an ongoing research program. The website is an accessible overview, not a substitute for the complete scholarly arguments, examples, and constraints presented in the research materials.
