The Research

Grammatical Transformation as Operatorial Structure

This research proposes the Δ operator as a unified formal framework for describing grammatical transformations. It distinguishes macrostructural correspondences from microstructural organization and classifies transformation patterns into eight operatorial regimes.

The approach aims to support comparative linguistic description and further investigation into formal representation and computational applications.

Core Concepts

Theoretical Framework

01 / MACROSTRUCTURE

Relations Between Configurations

The macrostructural level represents formal correspondences between grammatical configurations.

02 / MICROSTRUCTURE

Internal Transformations

The microstructural level describes the internal changes involved in grammatical transformation.

The architecture explores whether a unified operatorial notation can describe different kinds of grammatical transformation while preserving the distinctions between them. These proposals are part of an ongoing research program.
