The Δ Architecture of Grammatical Transformation

Ion Valentin Ciocioc · Independent research

A comparative formal framework for describing structural relations and interventions in linguistic configurations.

ORCID profileGitHub repository

Research overview

The Δ architecture distinguishes macrostructural descriptions of relations between configurations from microstructural specifications of a relational nucleus (R₀) and affected dependent material. Its regimes are comparative classifications rather than claims that linguistic theories share one explanatory mechanism.

Eight operatorial regimes

Δ(→) · Δ(+) · Δ(><) · Δ(−/+) · Δ(>/<) · Δ(−) · Δ(~) · Δ(±)

Directional correspondence, integration, semantic or grammatical reorientation, correlated replacement, relational coupling, elimination, positional reorganization, and cumulative paradigmatic organization.
