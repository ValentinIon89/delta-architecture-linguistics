Abstract

This work proposes the Δ operator as a unified formal model for grammatical transformation. It offers a macro–micro architecture, eight operatorial regimes, and a cross-linguistic analytical framework.

The approach supports research in linguistic theory, computational modelling, and language education.

About the Research

The Δ operator provides a single, formally defined mechanism for grammatical transformation, applicable across languages and architectural levels.

The framework distinguishes macrostructural correspondences from microstructural organization and proposes eight operatorial regimes.

It offers a unified perspective on morphological, syntactic, and hierarchical alternations, with formal representations and illustrative analyses.
