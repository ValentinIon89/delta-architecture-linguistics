About the Research

The Δ architecture explores a unified operatorial notation for grammatical transformation across different linguistic configurations.

Its macrostructural level represents relations between configurations, while its microstructural level describes their internal organization and transformation.

The framework distinguishes eight operatorial regimes and provides formal representations, diagnostic procedures, and illustrative analyses.
